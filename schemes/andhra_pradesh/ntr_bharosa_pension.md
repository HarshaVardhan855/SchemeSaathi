# NTR Bharosa Pension (Andhra Pradesh) — Enhanced Social Security Pension
**Status: CONTINUED under TDP Government (June 2024+) — NTR is TDP founder**

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Andhra Pradesh
- **Department:** Department of Panchayat Raj & Rural Development
- **Official Portal:** https://navasakam.ap.gov.in/ / https://ap.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://navasakam.ap.gov.in/static/guidelines/NTR_Bharosa_Pension_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/ntrbharosapension

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Age 60+ (Old Age) OR Widow 18+ OR Disabled 18+ OR CKDU patient OR HIV+ OR Transgender
- [ ] Resident of Andhra Pradesh
- [ ] Family annual income ≤ ₹1,50,000 (rural) / ₹2,00,000 (urban)
- [ ] Not receiving any other pension (central/state)

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Government employee (serving/retired)
- [ ] Income tax payee
- [ ] Owns >2.5 acres irrigated / >5 acres non-irrigated land
- [ ] Owns 4-wheeler (except commercial)
- [ ] Receiving any other pension

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Old Age (60+):** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] **Widow (18+):** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] **Disabled (18+, ≥40%):** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] **CKDU Patient:** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] **HIV+:** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] **Transgender:** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] **Weavers/Toddy Tappers/Artisans:** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] **Dappu Artists:** ₹3,000/month
- [ ] Replaces NSAP pensions (higher amount)
- [ ] **Note:** Continued by TDP government (named after NTR, TDP founder)

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Age Proof (Aadhaar / Birth certificate)
3. Disability Certificate (≥40%) / Medical Certificate (CKDU/HIV)
4. Widow: Death Certificate of Husband
5. Income Certificate (MeeSeva)
6. Bank Passbook (first page with IFSC)
7. Ration Card (for family income)
8. Passport-size Photo

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://navasakam.ap.gov.in/ (NTR Bharosa Pension tab)
- **Offline:** Village Secretariat / Ward Secretariat / MeeSeva
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Name, Father/Husband Name
  - Date of Birth / Age
  - Pension Category (Old Age/Widow/Disabled/CKDU/HIV/Transgender)
  - Disability Percentage (if applicable)
  - Village, Mandal, District
  - Bank Account Details
  - Mobile Number

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹3,000/month (all categories)
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Mode:** DBT to bank account (1st of every month)

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Already getting NSAP ₹200?** → A: Auto-migrated to NTR Bharosa ₹3,000; no re-application needed
- **Q: Disability <40%?** → A: Not eligible for disability pension; check other categories
- **Q: Widow age 35?** → A: Eligible - widow pension from 18+
- **Q: Transgender certificate?** → A: Certificate from District Magistrate / Transgender Welfare Board
- **Q: How to check status?** → A: navasakam.ap.gov.in → Pension Status → enter Aadhaar
- **Q: Is this scheme continued?** → A: YES - continued by TDP government (NTR = TDP founder)
