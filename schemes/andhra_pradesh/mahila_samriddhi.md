# Mahila Samriddhi / Women Empowerment Scheme (Andhra Pradesh)
**Replaces: YSR Cheyutha | Current as of TDP Government (June 2024+)**

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Andhra Pradesh
- **Department:** Department of Women Development & Child Welfare / BC Welfare / Minority Welfare
- **Official Portal:** https://navasakam.ap.gov.in/ / https://ap.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** Pending — check https://ap.gov.in/ for latest GO
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mahilasamriddhi

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Woman belonging to BC / SC / ST / Minority community
- [ ] Age 45 to 60 years (as on 01-06 of current year)
- [ ] Resident of Andhra Pradesh
- [ ] Family annual income ≤ ₹2,50,000
- [ ] Not a government employee / pensioner

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Age <45 or >60
- [ ] Income >₹2.5 lakh/year
- [ ] Government employee (serving/retired)
- [ ] Income tax payee
- [ ] Already availed similar benefit

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Benefit:** ₹75,000 over 4 years (₹18,750 per year)
- [ ] Paid in 4 annual installments
- [ ] Linked to bank account for DBT
- [ ] Can be used for livelihood activities (livestock, shop, tailoring, etc.)
- [ ] **Note:** Scheme guidelines under revision by new government — verify latest GO

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Caste Certificate (BC/SC/ST/Minority)
3. Age Proof (Aadhaar / Birth certificate)
4. Income Certificate (MeeSeva / Tahsildar)
5. Bank Passbook (first page with IFSC)
6. Passport-size Photo
7. Ration Card (for family verification)

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://navasakam.ap.gov.in/ (Mahila Samriddhi tab)
- **Offline:** Village Secretariat / Ward Secretariat / MeeSeva
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Name, Father/Husband Name
  - Date of Birth / Age
  - Caste, Sub-caste
  - Village, Mandal, District
  - Bank Account Details
  - Mobile Number
  - Livelihood Activity Proposed

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹75,000 total (₹18,750 × 4 years)
- **Frequency:** Annual installment
- **Mode:** DBT to bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: General category woman?** → A: Not eligible - only BC/SC/ST/Minority
- **Q: Age 44 turning 45 this year?** → A: Eligible if 45 as on 01-06 of current year
- **Q: Already have shop/business?** → A: Eligible - can expand existing activity
- **Q: How to use funds?** → A: Any income-generating activity (livestock, retail, tailoring, etc.)
- **Q: Widow eligible?** → A: Yes, if meets age/caste/income criteria
- **Q: Is this YSR Cheyutha?** → A: Renamed by TDP government; same benefit structure
