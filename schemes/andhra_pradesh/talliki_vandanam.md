# Talliki Vandanam (Andhra Pradesh) — Mother's Incentive for School Education
**Replaces: Amma Vodi | Current as of TDP Government (June 2024+)**

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Andhra Pradesh
- **Department:** Department of School Education
- **Official Portal:** https://navasakam.ap.gov.in/ / https://ap.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** Pending — check https://ap.gov.in/ for latest GO
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/tallikivandanam

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Mother/guardian of child studying in Class 1 to 12
- [ ] Child enrolled in Government / ZP / Municipal / Aided / Model school
- [ ] Resident of Andhra Pradesh
- [ ] Child has minimum 75% attendance (previous year)

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Child in private unaided school
- [ ] Family income >₹2,50,000/year (some relaxations)
- [ ] Child dropped out / irregular attendance

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Benefit:** ₹15,000 per year per mother (regardless of number of children)
- [ ] Paid in one installment (usually January)
- [ ] Mother must be alive and guardian of child
- [ ] If mother deceased, father/guardian eligible
- [ ] **Note:** Scheme guidelines under revision by new government — verify latest GO

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card (Mother)
2. Child's Aadhaar / Birth Certificate
3. School Bonafide Certificate (from HM)
4. Ration Card (for family verification)
5. Bank Passbook (Mother's, first page with IFSC)
6. Attendance Certificate (75%+)

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://navasakam.ap.gov.in/ (Talliki Vandanam tab)
- **Offline:** School HM / Village Secretariat / Ward Secretariat
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Mother's Aadhaar
  - Mother's Name, Age
  - Child's Name, Class, School UDISE Code
  - School Name, Mandal, District
  - Mother's Bank Account Details
  - Mobile Number

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹15,000 per year
- **Frequency:** Annual (one installment)
- **Mode:** DBT to mother's bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Two children in school?** → A: Still ₹15,000 per mother (not per child)
- **Q: Mother deceased?** → A: Father/legal guardian eligible with death certificate
- **Q: Private school?** → A: Not eligible - only Govt/ZP/Municipal/Aided/Model schools
- **Q: Attendance <75%?** → A: Not eligible; ensure regular attendance
- **Q: How to check status?** → A: navasakam.ap.gov.in → Talliki Vandanam → enter Aadhaar
- **Q: Is this Amma Vodi?** → A: Renamed by TDP government; same benefit structure
