# Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) — Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Central
- **Department:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare / National Health Authority
- **Official Portal:** https://pmjay.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://pmjay.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-05/PM-JAY_Operational_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmjay

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Family listed in SECC 2011 (Socio-Economic Caste Census) deprivation criteria
- [ ] OR covered under state-specific expansion categories
- [ ] Indian citizen residing in India

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Family owning motorized 2/3/4-wheeler (except for commercial use)
- [ ] Family owning mechanized farming equipment (tractor, harvester)
- [ ] Family with Kisan Credit Card limit >₹50,000
- [ ] Government employee (regular) or family member
- [ ] Income tax payee
- [ ] Professional tax payee
- [ ] Family owning >5 acres irrigated land
- [ ] Family owning >10 acres non-irrigated land
- [ ] Family owning residential property >1000 sq ft in urban area

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] AP: Dr. YSR Aarogyasri covers additional families (income <₹5L/yr)
- [ ] TS: Aarogyasri covers additional categories
- [ ] Some states have expanded coverage beyond SECC 2011

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card (mandatory for e-KYC)
2. Ration Card (for family verification)
3. Mobile Number (for OTP)
4. Address Proof (if not in Aadhaar)
5. Caste Certificate (if applicable for state schemes)

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://mera.pmjay.gov.in/ (eligibility check)
- **Offline:** Empaneled Hospital / CSC / District Kiosk / Arogya Mitra
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Mobile Number (OTP)
  - State, District
  - Search by: Name / Ration Card / Mobile / HHD Number
  - e-KYC at hospital

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹5,00,000 per family per year
- **Frequency:** Annual (floater basis - any family member)
- **Mode:** Cashless at empaneled hospitals (public & private)

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Not in SECC 2011 but poor?** → A: Check state-specific expansion (AP: YSR Aarogyasri, TS: Aarogyasri). Visit CSC for survey.
- **Q: How to check eligibility?** → A: Visit mera.pmjay.gov.in → enter mobile/ration card → check status
- **Q: Pre-existing conditions covered?** → A: Yes, all pre-existing conditions covered from day 1
- **Q: Which hospitals?** → A: Check "Find Hospital" on pmjay.gov.in or ask Arogya Mitra at hospital
- **Q: Family size limit?** → A: No limit - all family members in same ration card covered
- **Q: Maternity benefits?** → A: Normal delivery ₹9,000, C-section ₹15,000 (under PM-JAY + JSY)
- **Q: Portability?** → A: Yes, can avail treatment in any empaneled hospital across India
