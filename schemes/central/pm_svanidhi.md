# PM-SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi)

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Central
- **Department:** Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs
- **Official Portal:** https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/assets/docs/PM-SVANidhi_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmsvanidhi

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Street vendor engaged in vending in urban areas
- [ ] Possess Certificate of Vending / Identity Card issued by ULB
- [ ] OR possess Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from ULB
- [ ] Age 18 years or above
- [ ] Indian citizen

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Not a street vendor (shop owners, fixed establishments excluded)
- [ ] Already availed 3 loans under PM-SVANidhi
- [ ] Defaulter on previous PM-SVANidhi loans
- [ ] Income tax payee (for 3rd tranche)

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Tranche 1:** ₹10,000 loan (no collateral, 7% interest subsidy)
- [ ] **Tranche 2:** ₹20,000 loan (after repaying 1st)
- [ ] **Tranche 3:** ₹50,000 loan (after repaying 2nd, digital transactions mandatory)
- [ ] Interest subsidy: 7% per annum (credited quarterly)
- [ ] Digital payment incentive: ₹100/month (max ₹1,200/yr)
- [ ] Vending zone certificate / LoR mandatory

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Certificate of Vending / Identity Card (issued by ULB)
3. OR Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from ULB
4. Bank Passbook (first page with IFSC)
5. Mobile Number (linked to Aadhaar)
6. Passport-size Photo

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/ (vendor login)
- **Offline:** CSC / ULB Office / Bank Branch
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Mobile Number (OTP)
  - Name, Father/Spouse Name
  - Date of Birth / Age
  - Gender
  - Category (General/SC/ST/OBC)
  - State, District, ULB
  - Vending Zone / Location
  - Certificate of Vending / LoR Number
  - Bank Account Details
  - Loan Amount Requested (₹10k/20k/50k)

## Benefit Details
- **Tranche 1:** ₹10,000 loan
- **Tranche 2:** ₹20,000 loan
- **Tranche 3:** ₹50,000 loan
- **Interest Subsidy:** 7% per annum
- **Digital Incentive:** ₹100/month for digital transactions
- **Frequency:** Loan tenure 12 months (extendable)
- **Mode:** DBT to bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: No Certificate of Vending?** → A: Get Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from ULB; or apply for survey
- **Q: Rural street vendor?** → A: Only urban areas (municipal corporation/council/nagar panchayat)
- **Q: How to repay?** → A: EMIs deducted from bank account; digital payments earn ₹100/month cashback
- **Q: Defaulted on previous loan?** → A: Not eligible for next tranche until cleared
- **Q: Woman vendor priority?** → A: No specific quota but encouraged; SHG members get support
- **Q: Can apply without smartphone?** → A: Yes, through CSC / bank branch / ULB office
