# NSAP Widow Pension (Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme - IGNWPS)

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Central
- **Department:** Ministry of Rural Development
- **Official Portal:** https://nsap.nic.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://nsap.nic.in/guidelines/IGNWPS_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/ignwp

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Widowed (husband deceased)
- [ ] Age 40 to 79 years
- [ ] Belong to Below Poverty Line (BPL) household
- [ ] Not receiving any other pension (central/state)
- [ ] Indian citizen residing in the state

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Already receiving widow/old age/disability pension from any government
- [ ] Government employee (serving/retired) or family pensioner
- [ ] Income tax payee
- [ ] Remarried (widow pension stops on remarriage)
- [ ] Owns >2.5 acres irrigated / >5 acres non-irrigated land (varies by state)

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Central share:** ₹300/month (age 40-79)
- [ ] **AP (NTR Bharosa):** ₹3,000/month (enhanced)
- [ ] **TS (Aasara):** ₹2,016/month (age 40-79), ₹3,016/month (80+)
- [ ] BPL identification via SECC 2011 or state BPL list
- [ ] Aadhaar mandatory for DBT

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Death Certificate of Husband (mandatory)
3. Age Proof (Birth certificate / School certificate / Aadhaar)
4. BPL Card / Ration Card (BPL category)
5. Bank Passbook (first page with IFSC)
6. Passport-size Photo
7. Affidavit (widowed, not remarried, not receiving other pension)

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** Varies by state (AP: navasakam.ap.gov.in, TS: aasara.telangana.gov.in)
- **Offline:** Gram Panchayat / Mandal Office / MeeSeva / CSC
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Name, Husband's Name
  - Date of Birth / Age
  - Husband's Death Date
  - Aadhaar Number
  - BPL Card/Ration Card Number
  - Bank Account Details
  - Mobile Number
  - Address (Village, Mandal, District)

## Benefit Details
- **Amount (Central):** ₹300/month (age 40-79)
- **Amount (AP - NTR Bharosa):** ₹3,000/month
- **Amount (TS - Aasara):** ₹2,016/month (40-79), ₹3,016/month (80+)
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Mode:** DBT to bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Death certificate not available?** → A: Apply for death certificate at Registrar of Births & Deaths; interim: FIR/panchnama accepted in some states
- **Q: Remarried but husband died again?** → A: Not eligible - remarriage disqualifies permanently
- **Q: Age 39 but widowed?** → A: Not eligible until age 40; check state schemes for younger widows
- **Q: Already getting state widow pension?** → A: Cannot get both; choose higher amount (usually state)
- **Q: Pension for children?** → A: Separate scheme - National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS) gives ₹20,000 on death of primary breadwinner
