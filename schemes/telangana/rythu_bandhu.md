# Rythu Bandhu (Telangana) — Farmer Investment Support Scheme
**Status: CONTINUED under Congress Government (Dec 2023+) — Congress promised continuation**

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Telangana
- **Department:** Agriculture Department, Govt of Telangana
- **Official Portal:** https://rythubandhu.telangana.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://rythubandhu.telangana.gov.in/assets/docs/Rythu_Bandhu_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/rythubandhu

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Farmer owning agricultural land in Telangana
- [ ] Land recorded in Dharani portal (Pattadar Passbook)
- [ ] Any land extent (no minimum/maximum)

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Institutional landholders (trusts, societies, companies)
- [ ] Government employees (serving/retired Group A/B)
- [ ] Income tax payee (last assessment year)
- [ ] Professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CA) registered with professional bodies

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Benefit:** ₹10,000 per acre per year (₹5,000 per season)
- [ ] Two seasons: Kharif (₹5,000/acre) + Rabi (₹5,000/acre)
- [ ] No ceiling on land extent
- [ ] Tenant farmers NOT eligible (only landowners with Pattadar Passbook)
- [ ] Land must be in Dharani (Pattadar Passbook)
- [ ] **Note:** Continued by Congress government with same structure

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Pattadar Passbook (Dharani)
3. Bank Passbook (first page with IFSC)
4. Mobile Number

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://rythubandhu.telangana.gov.in/ (auto-credited if Dharani seeded)
- **Offline:** Agriculture Office / Rythu Vedika / Mandal Office
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Pattadar Passbook Number
  - Survey Numbers
  - Extent (acres/guntas)
  - Bank Account Details

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹10,000 per acre per year
- **Breakdown:** ₹5,000 per acre per season (Kharif + Rabi)
- **Frequency:** Twice a year
- **Mode:** DBT to bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Tenant farmer eligible?** → A: No - only landowners with Pattadar Passbook in Dharani
- **Q: Land in multiple districts?** → A: Single payment covers all land in Telangana
- **Q: Joint pattadar passbook?** → A: Amount split among pattadars as per share
- **Q: How to check status?** → A: rythubandhu.telangana.gov.in → enter Aadhaar/Pattadar Passbook
- **Q: Dharani not updated?** → A: Update land record at MeeSeva first
- **Q: Is this scheme continued?** → A: YES - Congress government continued it
