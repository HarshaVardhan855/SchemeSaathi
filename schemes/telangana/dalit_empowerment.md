## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC)
- [ ] Resident of Telangana
- [ ] Age 18 to 60 years
- [ ] Family annual income ≤ ₹2,50,000
- [ ] Not a government employee

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Not SC community
- [ ] Age >60 or <18
- [ ] Government employee (serving/retired)
- [ ] Income tax payee
- [ ] Already availed similar benefit

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Benefit:** ₹10,00,000 per family (unit cost) for income-generating activity
- [ ] Activities: Dairy, sheep/goat, retail, tailoring, auto-rickshaw, fisheries, etc.
- [ ] Selected through Gram Sabha / District Committee
- [ ] 100% grant (no repayment)
- [ ] Implemented in phases (constituency-wise)
- [ ] **Note:** Restructured by Congress government — guidelines under revision

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card (Applicant)
2. Caste Certificate (SC)
3. Age Proof (Aadhaar / Birth certificate)
4. Income Certificate (MeeSeva)
5. Bank Passbook (first page with IFSC)
6. Ration Card
7. Passport-size Photo
8. Project Proposal / Activity Plan

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://scdevelopment.telangana.gov.in/ (Dalit Empowerment tab)
- **Offline:** Village Secretariat / MRO Office / District SC Development Office
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Name, Father/Husband Name
  - Date of Birth / Age
  - Caste, Sub-caste
  - Village, Mandal, District
  - Proposed Activity
  - Estimated Cost
  - Bank Account Details

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹10,00,000 per family (unit cost)
- **Frequency:** One-time grant
- **Mode:** DBT to bank account / Direct to vendor

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Multiple SC families in one house?** → A: One grant per family (separate ration card)
- **Q: Woman-headed household?** → A: Priority given to women applicants
- **Q: What activities allowed?** → A: Dairy, sheep/goat rearing, retail shop, tailoring, auto-rickshaw, fishing, etc.
- **Q: How are beneficiaries selected?** → A: Gram Sabha identifies → Committee verifies → District approves
- **Q: Can apply if not selected in current phase?** → A: Wait for next phase in constituency
- **Q: Is this Dalit Bandhu?** → A: Restructured by Congress government; same core benefit, new guidelines
