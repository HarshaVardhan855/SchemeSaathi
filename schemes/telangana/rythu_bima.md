# Rythu Bima (Telangana) — Farmer Life Insurance Scheme
**Status: CONTINUED under Congress Government (Dec 2023+)**

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Telangana
- **Department:** Agriculture Department, Govt of Telangana
- **Official Portal:** https://rythubima.telangana.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://rythubima.telangana.gov.in/assets/docs/Rythu_Bima_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/rythubima

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Farmer aged 18 to 59 years
- [ ] Enrolled in Rythu Bandhu (landowner with Pattadar Passbook)
- [ ] Resident of Telangana

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Age >59 years
- [ ] Not enrolled in Rythu Bandhu
- [ ] Tenant farmer (not landowner)

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Cover:** ₹5,00,000 life insurance
- [ ] **Premium:** Fully paid by Government of Telangana
- [ ] **Coverage:** Natural death, accidental death, partial/permanent disability
- [ ] **Nominee:** Spouse / legal heir
- [ ] Auto-enrolled if in Rythu Bandhu
- [ ] **Note:** Continued by Congress government

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Pattadar Passbook (Dharani)
3. Nominee Aadhaar (optional but recommended)
4. Bank Passbook

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://rythubima.telangana.gov.in/ (auto-enrollment)
- **Offline:** Agriculture Office / Rythu Vedika
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Pattadar Passbook Number
  - Nominee Name, Age, Relation
  - Nominee Aadhaar

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹5,00,000 (sum assured)
- **Premium:** ₹0 (government pays)
- **Frequency:** Annual renewal (auto)
- **Mode:** Claim paid to nominee bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Separate application needed?** → A: No - auto-enrolled with Rythu Bandhu
- **Q: Tenant farmer covered?** → A: No - only Rythu Bandhu beneficiaries (landowners)
- **Q: Accidental death extra?** → A: Same ₹5 lakh for natural/accidental death
- **Q: Disability cover?** → A: Partial disability ₹2.5L, permanent total disability ₹5L
- **Q: How to claim?** → A: Nominee contacts Agriculture Officer + submits death certificate
- **Q: Is this scheme continued?** → A: YES - Congress government continued it
