# Aasara Pension (Telangana) — Enhanced Social Security Pension
**Status: CONTINUED & ENHANCED under Congress Government (Dec 2023+)**

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Telangana
- **Department:** Department of Panchayat Raj & Rural Development
- **Official Portal:** https://aasara.telangana.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://aasara.telangana.gov.in/assets/docs/Aasara_Pension_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/aasara

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Resident of Telangana
- [ ] Age 57+ (Old Age) OR Widow 18+ OR Disabled 18+ (≥40%) OR HIV+ OR Transgender OR Toddy Tapper/Weaver/Beedi Worker/Artisan
- [ ] Family annual income ≤ ₹1,50,000 (rural) / ₹2,00,000 (urban)
- [ ] Not receiving any other pension

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Government employee (serving/retired)
- [ ] Income tax payee
- [ ] Owns >3 acres irrigated / >5 acres non-irrigated land
- [ ] Owns 4-wheeler (except commercial)
- [ ] Receiving any other pension (central/state)

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **Old Age (57+):** ₹2,016/month (enhanced from ₹1,000)
- [ ] **Widow (18+):** ₹2,016/month
- [ ] **Disabled (18+, ≥40%):** ₹3,016/month
- [ ] **HIV+:** ₹3,016/month
- [ ] **Transgender:** ₹3,016/month
- [ ] **Toddy Tapper/Weaver/Beedi Worker/Artisan (57+):** ₹2,016/month
- [ ] **Filaria/Lepra/Stone Crusher:** ₹3,016/month
- [ ] Replaces NSAP (higher amounts)
- [ ] **Note:** Enhanced by Congress government

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Age Proof (Aadhaar / Birth certificate)
3. Disability Certificate (≥40%) / Medical Certificate (HIV/Filaria)
4. Widow: Death Certificate of Husband
5. Income Certificate (MeeSeva)
6. Bank Passbook (first page with IFSC)
7. Ration Card (for family income)
8. Passport-size Photo

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://aasara.telangana.gov.in/
- **Offline:** Gram Panchayat / Municipality / MeeSeva
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Aadhaar Number
  - Name, Father/Husband Name
  - Date of Birth / Age
  - Pension Category
  - Disability Percentage (if applicable)
  - Village, Mandal, District
  - Bank Account Details
  - Mobile Number

## Benefit Details
- **Old Age/Widow/Toddy Tapper:** ₹2,016/month
- **Disabled/HIV/Transgender/Filaria:** ₹3,016/month
- **Frequency:** Monthly (1st of every month)
- **Mode:** DBT to bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Age 57 eligible?** → A: Yes, Telangana old age from 57 (central is 60)
- **Q: Already getting NSAP?** → A: Auto-migrated to Aasara with higher amount
- **Q: Disability <40%?** → A: Not eligible for disability category; check old age if 57+
- **Q: How to check status?** → A: aasara.telangana.gov.in → Pension Status → enter Aadhaar
- **Q: Is this scheme continued?** → A: YES - Congress government continued & enhanced it
