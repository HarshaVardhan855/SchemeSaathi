# Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak (Telangana) — Marriage Grant for SC/ST/BC/Minority Brides
**Status: CONTINUED under Congress Government (Dec 2023+)**

## Basic Info
- **Jurisdiction:** Telangana
- **Department:** Scheduled Castes Development / Tribal Welfare / BC Welfare / Minority Welfare
- **Official Portal:** https://kalyanalakshmi.telangana.gov.in/
- **Guidelines PDF:** https://kalyanalakshmi.telangana.gov.in/assets/docs/Kalyana_Lakshmi_Guidelines.pdf
- **MyScheme/State Portal Page:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/kalyanalakshmi

## Eligibility Rules (Structured)

### Must Have (ALL required)
- [ ] Bride belonging to SC / ST / BC / Minority community
- [ ] Resident of Telangana
- [ ] Age 18+ at time of marriage
- [ ] Family annual income ≤ ₹2,00,000
- [ ] Marriage registered under Hindu Marriage Act / Special Marriage Act

### Must NOT Have (ANY disqualifies)
- [ ] Bride age <18 (child marriage)
- [ ] Family income >₹2 lakh/year
- [ ] Bride not from eligible community
- [ ] Marriage not registered

### Conditional / State-Specific
- [ ] **SC/ST:** Kalyana Lakshmi — ₹1,00,116
- [ ] **BC:** Kalyana Lakshmi — ₹1,00,116
- [ ] **Minority:** Shaadi Mubarak — ₹1,00,116
- [ ] One-time grant at time of marriage
- [ ] Application within 3 months of marriage
- [ ] **Note:** Continued by Congress government

## Required Documents (Exact Names)
1. Bride's Aadhaar Card
2. Groom's Aadhaar Card
3. Caste Certificate (SC/ST/BC/Minority)
4. Age Proof (Birth certificate / SSC / Aadhaar)
5. Marriage Registration Certificate
6. Income Certificate (MeeSeva)
7. Bank Passbook (Bride's, first page with IFSC)
8. Wedding Invitation Card
9. Passport-size Photos (Bride & Groom)

## Application Process
- **Online URL:** https://kalyanalakshmi.telangana.gov.in/
- **Offline:** MeeSeva / MRO Office / District Welfare Office
- **Key Form Fields:**
  - Bride's Aadhaar, Name, Age, Caste
  - Groom's Aadhaar, Name, Age
  - Marriage Date, Registration Number
  - Bride's Bank Account Details
  - Parents' Names
  - Village, Mandal, District

## Benefit Details
- **Amount:** ₹1,00,116 (one lakh sixteen)
- **Frequency:** One-time
- **Mode:** DBT to bride's bank account

## FAQ / Edge Cases (for RAG)
- **Q: Inter-caste marriage?** → A: Eligible if bride from SC/ST/BC/Minority
- **Q: Love marriage without registration?** → A: Must register marriage first
- **Q: Bride from AP, groom from TS?** → A: Bride must be Telangana resident
- **Q: Second marriage?** → A: Not eligible (only first marriage)
- **Q: How to check status?** → A: kalyanalakshmi.telangana.gov.in → Status → enter Aadhaar
- **Q: Is this scheme continued?** → A: YES - Congress government continued it
